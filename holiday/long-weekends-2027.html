Holidays on a Saturday

Labour Day, Sat 1 May. Schools are off on Mon 3 May.

Christmas Day, Sat 25 Dec.

When a holiday falls on a Saturday there is no automatic Monday off. Employers give a day off in lieu or a day's pay instead, depending on your contract.

Quick answers

How many long weekends does Singapore have in 2027?

Five: New Year's Day (1–3 Jan), Chinese New Year (6–8 Feb), Good Friday (26–28 Mar), Hari Raya Haji (15–17 May) and National Day (7–9 Aug).

When should I take leave in 2027 in Singapore?

The best value is the week of Hari Raya Haji and Vesak Day: take Tue 18, Wed 19 and Fri 21 May for 9 days off. For Hari Raya Puasa, take Thu 11 and Fri 12 Mar for 5 days off.
