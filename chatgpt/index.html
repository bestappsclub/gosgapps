GoSG for ChatGPT

Ask ChatGPT about Singapore buses and holidays, and get live, official answers.

What you can ask

“When's the 190 coming at Orchard Stn?” · “Bus stops near Tampines Hub” · “When's the last 36 tonight?” · “Long weekends in 2027?” · “When are the June school holidays?” · “Next public holiday?”

Where the answers come from

Bus arrival times come live from LTA DataMall. First and last bus times follow the operators' timetables. Public holidays come from the Ministry of Manpower (MOM) and school holidays from the Ministry of Education (MOE).

Privacy

GoSG for ChatGPT has no accounts and stores nothing about you.

What we receive: only what ChatGPT sends to answer your question: a bus stop code or name, a bus number, a year, or a location if you ask for stops nearby. We don't receive your name, email or ChatGPT conversation.

How we use it: to look up the answer. A stop code is sent to our own server, which asks LTA DataMall for live times. A location is only used to find the nearest stops and is not stored or logged.

What we keep: short-lived technical logs of which tool was used, the stop or year asked about, whether it worked and how long it took, kept for a few days to fix problems and count usage. We don't sell or share this data, and we don't use it for ads.

Our hosting: Cloudflare (the server) and Google Cloud (the bus data service). Like any website, they process your request's IP address to deliver it; we don't store it.
