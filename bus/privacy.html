GoSG Bus Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

GoSG: Bus Timing & Arrival ("GoSG Bus", "the app") shows live Singapore bus arrival times using data from the Land Transport Authority (LTA) DataMall. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location while you use it to find bus stops near you. Your location is used on your device to pick nearby stops. Your GPS coordinates are not sent to us or stored on our servers.

If you turn on a get-off alert, the app also uses your location in the background, only until that trip ends: at your stop, if you leave the route, or after 2 hours at most. iOS shows the blue location indicator while it runs. This is done on your device too; your location is not sent to us.

Favourites and settings

Your favourite stops, recent searches, settings and the record of which buses and stops you look at (used to suggest questions in the Ask tab) are stored only on your device.

Bus data requests

To show arrival times, the app asks our server (Google Firebase, Singapore region) for the bus stop or service you are looking at. The server fetches the data from LTA DataMall.

Ask tab (AI answers)

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, AI answers are written on your device by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent anywhere for those answers, and no account is needed.

Cloud AI answers need a free account (see below). When you ask a question that needs one, the app sends your question text, the bus and stop details needed to answer it (for example stop codes and arrival times), your app language and your account sign-in token to our server. The server passes the question and those details to our AI provider, DeepSeek, to write the answer; DeepSeek does not receive your name, email or account. We keep a monthly count of cloud answers for each account to apply the limit of 10 a month; these counts are deleted automatically. Please do not include personal information in your questions.

Account (Sign in with Apple or Google)

Bus times, widgets, alerts, the map and on-device AI work without an account. To use cloud AI answers you can sign in with Apple or Google. We use Google Firebase Authentication for this. We receive a user ID and, if you share it, your email address (with Apple you can hide it behind a private relay address) and name. We use them only to sign you in: we don't show them to anyone, use them for marketing or sell them.

While you're signed in, the questions you ask in Ask and the answers shown are saved with your account so we can see which questions are useful and improve the answers. You can delete your account in Settings › Account › Delete account; this deletes the account, revokes Sign in with Apple, and deletes the saved questions.

Feedback

If you send feedback or report a problem, we receive your message, the app version, your device model, iOS version and language setting, and your email address only if you choose to give it. We use these only to reply and fix problems.

Diagnostics

The app uses New Relic to collect crash reports and performance data (such as app version, device model, iOS version and network timing). This data is not linked to your identity and is used only to keep the app working.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can turn off location access at any time in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. You can delete your account and saved questions in Settings › Account. To ask us to delete feedback you sent, email us.
