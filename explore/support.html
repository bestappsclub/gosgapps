Common questions

  • How do I get a stamp? Take or add a photo while you are at one of the places. The stamp appears in Explore › Places.
  • My photo isn't on the map. The photo needs a location. Turn on Location for GoSG Explore in iOS Settings, or pick photos from your library that have one.
  • The AI got it wrong. Open the photo, tap ⋯ and choose Rename, Change type, Identify again or Replace photo.
  • How many scans do I get? 1 free AI identification a day. Pro allows up to 70 a week. Adding photos to your journey without AI is always free.
  • Restore a subscription: Settings (gear icon) › Restore purchases.
  • Sync between devices: Settings › iCloud sync, signed in to the same Apple Account.
