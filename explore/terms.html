What GoSG Explore Does

GoSG Explore lists places to visit in Singapore, lets you collect stamps and keep photos of your visits, and uses artificial intelligence to suggest what is shown in a photo. Answers are generated automatically and can be wrong. Place details, opening hours and links may change; check the official website before you go.

Safety: Never touch, feed or eat a wild animal, plant, mushroom or berry based on the app. Keep a safe distance from wildlife, stay on marked paths and follow the rules of every park and attraction.
