Free places to explore in Singapore with kids

Free places, and what to look for

PlaceWhy go
Singapore Botanic GardensOrchids, swans, huge rain trees. The National Orchid Garden inside charges entry; the rest is free.
Gardens by the Bay (outdoor gardens)The Supertree Grove and outdoor gardens are free. The domes charge entry.
MacRitchie ReservoirForest trails and boardwalks with monkeys, monitor lizards and squirrels.
Bukit Timah Nature ReserveSingapore's highest hill, in old rainforest. Look for colugos.
Southern Ridges and Henderson WavesA long walk on bridges through the treetops, with hornbills and butterflies.
Sungei Buloh Wetland ReserveMudskippers, crabs, monitor lizards and sometimes a saltwater crocodile.
Jurong Lake GardensOtters, herons and a big nature playground in the west.
Fort Canning ParkThe tree tunnel, the spice garden and history in the city centre.
East Coast Park and Coney Island ParkBeaches, cycling and quiet casuarina woods.
Labrador Nature ReserveA rocky shore and old fort ruins by the sea.
Haw Par VillaThousands of statues from Chinese myths. The park is free; the museum inside charges entry.
Jewel Changi Airport (Rain Vortex)Watching the indoor waterfall is free.
Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Gelam, Katong and Tiong BahruTemples, murals, shophouses and hawker food.

Entry rules change sometimes, so check the official site for anything you plan around.

Turn free days out into a collection

All of these places have a stamp in GoSG Explore. Kids love filling the passport, and the camera turns any walk into a hunt: point it at a bird, a lizard or a flower and find out what it is.

Getting there

Most of these are an easy bus ride away. GoSG Bus shows live arrivals at your stop and the MRT map shows station exits.

Quick answers

What can you do for free in Singapore with kids?

Free options include the Botanic Gardens, the outdoor gardens at Gardens by the Bay, MacRitchie Reservoir, Bukit Timah Nature Reserve, the Southern Ridges, Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve, Jurong Lake Gardens, East Coast Park and the Rain Vortex at Jewel.
