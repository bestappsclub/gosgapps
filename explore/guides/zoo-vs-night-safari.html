Mandai

Singapore Zoo or Night Safari? How to choose

Both are in Mandai, but they're different days out. What you'll see at each, who each suits, and how to fit in Bird Paradise, River Wonders and Rainforest Wild.

The short answer

Singapore Zoo is a daytime visit: open, shady paths and big animals you can see clearly, like orangutans, white tigers, Asian elephants, proboscis monkeys and pygmy hippos. Night Safari starts in the evening: you see animals that are active after dark, like the Malayan tiger, fishing cat, slow loris, pangolin and spotted hyena, partly from a tram and partly on walking trails.

With young children, the Zoo is usually easier: daylight, splash areas, and you can leave whenever you like. Night Safari suits older kids and adults who don't mind a late night.

The other three Mandai parks

Mandai has five parks next to each other. Bird Paradise has walk-through aviaries with macaws, penguins, flamingos and hornbills. River Wonders has the giant pandas and red pandas, manatees and giant river otters. Rainforest Wild has treetop walks with gibbons, langurs and binturongs. Many families pair the Zoo with River Wonders on the same day, or Bird Paradise with Night Safari.

Tickets, park hours and bundles change, so check mandai.com before you go.

Make it a game for the kids

In GoSG Explore, each Mandai park has its own stamp, and collecting all five earns the Mandai badge. Point the camera at an animal and the app tells you what it is, with a few facts kids can read out. At the end of the day, replay your route on the map with your photos.

Getting there by bus

Mandai is served by public buses from several MRT stations. Open GoSG Bus at your stop to see which bus to Mandai comes first, and set a leave-now alert so you don't wait in the sun.

Quick answers

Which is better for young kids, Singapore Zoo or Night Safari?

Singapore Zoo is usually easier for young children: it's in daylight, you walk at your own pace and can leave any time. Night Safari runs late into the evening.

Can you do Singapore Zoo and Night Safari on the same day?

Yes, they are next to each other in Mandai. Many people do the Zoo in the afternoon and Night Safari after dinner. Check mandai.com for park hours and combined tickets.
