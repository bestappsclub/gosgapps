Rainy days

Rainy day in Singapore? Indoor places to take the kids

Indoor days out for when the afternoon storm arrives: aquariums, science, the Rain Vortex and the cool domes at Gardens by the Bay.

Indoor places that are worth the trip

Singapore Oceanarium (Sentosa): manta rays, sharks, jellyfish and sea turtles, all under cover.

Science Centre Singapore: hands-on exhibits, the Omni-Theatre and KidsSTOP for younger children.

Jewel Changi Airport: the Rain Vortex indoor waterfall, the Shiseido Forest Valley and Canopy Park. Watching the waterfall is free.

Gardens by the Bay: the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest are cool, dry glasshouses, with a waterfall, a baobab tree and pitcher plants.

National Gallery Singapore: big rooms, a rooftop garden and children's spaces. ArtScience Museum at Marina Bay Sands has hands-on exhibitions too.

Tips for wet weather

Singapore's rain often comes as a heavy storm in the afternoon and clears within an hour or two. Plan indoor places for the afternoon and keep outdoor plans for the morning. If there is lightning, NParks may close exposed trails and some outdoor areas until it passes.

Still collect your stamps

Every place above has a stamp in GoSG Explore, so a rainy day still fills the family passport. In the Oceanarium or the Cloud Forest, point the camera at a fish or a plant and the app names it.

Getting there without getting soaked

Check GoSG Bus before you step out: live arrivals mean less waiting at an open stop, and the MRT map shows which station exit is closest.

Quick answers

Where can kids go in Singapore when it rains?

Good indoor options are the Singapore Oceanarium on Sentosa, Science Centre Singapore, Jewel Changi Airport, the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest at Gardens by the Bay, and National Gallery Singapore.
