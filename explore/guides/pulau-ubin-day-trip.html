A day on Pulau Ubin with kids: how to go and what to see

Take the bumboat from Changi, rent bikes, walk the Chek Jawa boardwalk and look out for wild boar and hornbills. A simple plan for families.

Getting there

Bumboats to Pulau Ubin leave from Changi Point Ferry Terminal, next to Changi Village. Boats go when they're full, and the ride is about 10 to 15 minutes. Bring cash for the fare, and check the current fare at the jetty.

Several bus services end at Changi Village Bus Terminal. Open GoSG Bus to see which one comes first from your stop.

A simple plan for the day

Morning: rent bicycles in the village near the jetty (kids' bikes and bikes with child seats are usually available) and ride the main roads, which are quiet and mostly flat.

Late morning: head to Chek Jawa Wetlands on the east side. The boardwalk goes over mangroves and the sea, where you may see mudskippers, fiddler crabs, herons and seagrass. At low tide, more sea life is visible.

Lunch: the village has simple seafood and drinks stalls.

What to look for

Wild boar (keep your distance and never feed them), oriental pied hornbills, long-tailed macaques, mangroves, jackfruit trees and old kampong houses. Pulau Ubin is one of the last places in Singapore with a kampong village.

What to bring

Water, sunscreen, insect repellent, a hat, cash, and a bag to carry your rubbish back. Shade is patchy on the roads, so morning is cooler.

Two stamps in one trip

GoSG Explore has separate stamps for Pulau Ubin and Chek Jawa, so one trip fills two pages of the passport. Snap a hornbill or a crab and the app tells you what it is. Back home, your photos replay along the route you cycled.

Quick answers

How do you get to Pulau Ubin?

Take a bumboat from Changi Point Ferry Terminal near Changi Village. The ride is about 10 to 15 minutes and boats leave when they're full. Bring cash for the fare.

Is Pulau Ubin good for kids?

Yes. The roads are quiet for cycling, you can rent kids' bikes in the village, and the Chek Jawa boardwalk is an easy walk with lots of wildlife to spot.
