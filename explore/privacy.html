GoSG Explore Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

GoSG Explore helps you explore Singapore: collect stamps at famous places, identify the animals, plants and things you photograph, and replay your day on a map. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes and how long it is kept. GoSG Explore has no accounts and never asks for your name or email.

Information We Process

  • Photos you choose to identify: When you tap Identify, the photo (or the part inside the selection box) is sent to the GoSG Explore server. Nothing is sent before you tap Identify, and nothing is sent until you have agreed to AI processing in the app. Photos you add to your journey without identifying them are never sent.
  • The place name: If you are at one of the places in the app (for example Singapore Zoo), its name is sent with the photo so the answer can fit the place. Your exact coordinates are never sent.
  • Questions you ask: If you use Ask, your question and the identification it refers to are sent to the server to get an answer.
  • Technical data for abuse prevention: The server counts requests using your IP address and a random identifier created by the app on your device (not linked to your Apple Account). These counters are deleted automatically within 2 days.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your location on your iPhone to show you on the map, find the nearest place, and record where each photo was taken. The location of each photo is stored with that photo on your device (and in your iCloud, see below). We do not track your location in the background and we never receive it.

Third-Party AI Service

To produce an answer, the GoSG Explore server sends the photo (and, for Ask, your question) to DeepSeek, a third-party AI provider. DeepSeek processes this data only to generate the answer, under its own API terms and privacy policy. DeepSeek's servers may be located outside Singapore, including in the People's Republic of China. The server runs on Cloudflare, which processes network traffic to deliver the service.

You can withdraw consent at any time in GoSG Explore › Settings. Without it, the app cannot identify photos, but stamps, maps and your photo journey still work.

Data Retention

  • On our server: Photos, questions and answers are not stored after the answer is returned. Request counters are kept for up to 2 days.
  • On your device and iCloud: Your photos, stamps and results are stored on your iPhone. If iCloud sync is on (Settings), they are also kept in your own private iCloud account so your other devices can see them; we cannot access them. If you turn on "Save to Photos", photos you take are also saved to your photo library. Delete any photo in the app, or everything by deleting the app (and its iCloud data in iOS Settings).

Camera and Photo Library

The app uses the camera to take photos, and the photo picker to let you choose existing photos. The app only sees the photos you pick. It can read the time and location saved inside a picked photo to place it on your map.

Android

GoSG Explore for Android is free, with no in-app purchases, ads or accounts. It sends the same data to the same server as the iPhone app, as described above: the photo (or boxed part) you choose to identify, the name of the place in the app you are at, any question you ask, and an identifier for abuse prevention. On Android this identifier is a one-way hash of the Android ID for this app, so the raw ID never leaves your phone. The server allows a limited number of AI identifications per device each day.

  • Search the web: If you tap "Search the web" on a result, that photo is uploaded to our server for up to 1 hour so image search engines (such as Google Lens) can fetch it, and is then deleted automatically. Photos of faces are refused on your phone and never uploaded.
  • Location: Used on your phone only, while you use the app, to show you on the map, stamp the place you are at and tag your photos. It is never sent to us, and the app does not use location in the background.
  • Photos you add: The Android system file picker hands the app only the photos you choose; with the "media location" permission the app reads the place and time saved inside those photos. The app does not ask for access to your whole photo library. Photos you take can be saved to Pictures › GoSG Explore if that setting is on.
  • Backup instead of iCloud: Your finds, passport and small thumbnails are included in Android's own backup to your Google account (if backup is on in your phone's settings); full-size photos stay on your phone. We cannot access this backup.
  • Maps: Map images are loaded from OneMap (Singapore Land Authority). Like any website, OneMap receives your IP address and the map area being shown, but not your GPS position.
  • Deleting your data: Delete any photo in the app, or everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings. There is no account, so there is nothing else to delete on our server; request counters expire within 2 days.

Payments

On iPhone, subscriptions are processed by Apple. The Android app has no purchases. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

GoSG Explore is made for families. Children should use it with a parent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: the app has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no chat with other people.
